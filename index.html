Hunt Choco

Feed everyone, upset no one.

A hungry crowd. A wall of chocolate. Tap the right piece and the matching customer walks over, takes a bite and clears the way. Clear the whole wall to win the level.

🍎 Download on theApp Store ▶ Get it onGoogle Play

How to play

1Tap a chocolateOnly the bottom row of the wall is reachable. Pick a piece.
2Match the customerThe customer whose colour matches walks up and eats it. Wrong colour? They get upset.
3Clear the wallKeep the whole crowd happy and empty the wall to collect stars and coins.

Features

  • 110+ hand-crafted levels that get trickier as you go
  • Couples that eat together, hand in hand
  • Hammer, shuffle and freeze power-ups
  • Daily rewards and a 28-day reward track
  • 30 achievements to unlock
  • Online leaderboard and Game Center
  • Cosy, hand-painted chocolate shop art
  • Free to play, no account needed. English & Turkish.
